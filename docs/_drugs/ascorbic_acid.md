## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for ascorbic acid: "The exact mechanism of action of ascorbic acid for the treatment of symptoms and signs of scurvy (a disorder caused by severe deficiency in vitamin C) is unknown; however, administration of ascorbic acid in patients with scurvy is thought to restore the body pool of ascorbic acid." ([DailyMed: ascorbic acid prescribing information, Zydus Pharmaceuticals USA Inc.](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=f0b683f8-189b-4a3f-9ffe-f91df6d798b6), label version 2026-09-03; accessed 2026-11-19.) Ascorbic acid is generally known as an antioxidant and as a cofactor for hydroxylase enzymes involved in collagen stabilisation.

For this prediction, no mechanistic link has been identified. Non-syndromic esophageal malformation is a congenital structural condition, and there is no plausible way an antioxidant vitamin would correct a developmental anatomical defect. The very high TxGNN score is not backed by any trial or publication, and it should be read as a computational signal rather than evidence of benefit.
