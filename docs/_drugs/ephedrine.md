# Ephedrine: From Allergic Rhinitis to Nasal Cavity Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Ephedrine is a sympathomimetic agent originally used for upper respiratory and allergic conditions such as allergic rhinitis, vasomotor rhinitis, bronchitis and the common cold.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **nasal cavity disease**, but this is largely an extension of its existing rhinitis uses.
Support is limited to **0 clinical trials** and **2 publications**, both old and low in quality.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, ephedrine is an alpha- and beta-adrenergic agonist with indirect sympathomimetic activity. Vasoconstriction of the nasal mucosa reduces congestion and oedema, which is plausible for nasal cavity disease.

Nasal cavity disease overlaps heavily with the rhinitis and nasopharyngitis indications ephedrine already carries. The high TxGNN score probably reflects this closeness to existing uses, so this is an extension of known use rather than a genuinely new repurposing opportunity.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions (100 recorded; examples shown):**

  - Moderate: other sympathomimetics and beta-agonists (amphetamine, methylphenidate, salbutamol, formoterol, arformoterol, atomoxetine), beta-blockers (atenolol, acebutolol), and antidiabetic agents (metformin, acarbose, alogliptin, albiglutide)

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is plausible but largely duplicates existing rhinitis indications. The only support is two old, low-quality publications with no registered trials. The extensive cardiovascular, neurological and interaction risks have not been weighed against any demonstrated benefit.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Product Information (PI) warnings and contraindications for the Australian ARTG entry, including its dosage form and approved indications
- Mechanism of action data
- Modern controlled studies of ephedrine in nasal cavity disease, compared with current alternatives
- A clear statement of how nasal cavity disease differs from the already-listed rhinitis indications
- A safety assessment covering cardiovascular risk and the major drug interactions
