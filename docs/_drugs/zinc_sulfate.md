## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for this candidate. Based on known pharmacology, zinc sulfate is a mineral supplement whose primary established role is correcting zinc deficiency, but zinc ions also have well-documented direct astringent and anti-inflammatory effects on pharyngeal mucosa, and can inhibit rhinovirus replication and reduce release of pro-inflammatory cytokines. This is the classic mechanism underpinning the widespread over-the-counter use of zinc lozenges for sore throat and common cold symptom relief.

There is a plausible mechanistic bridge between zinc's role in mucosal immunity/tissue integrity (relevant to its use in deficiency states) and its topical/local action on inflamed pharyngeal tissue — these are related but distinct pharmacological actions of the same molecule (systemic replacement vs local mucosal effect). The strongest supporting evidence comes from a dedicated, adequately powered RCT (NCT02405832, n=87) testing zinc lozenges specifically for prevention of post-operative sore throat, which is a close clinical analogue of pharyngitis.

It is worth noting some of the supporting trials (COVID-19 studies) used zinc only as an adjunct alongside other agents (hydroxychloroquine, azithromycin, doxycycline) and are of limited relevance or statistical power (grade C). The most directly relevant evidence remains the sore-throat–specific trial and literature described below.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
The lead predicted indication (pharyngitis) is supported by L2-level evidence, anchored by a directly relevant, adequately sized RCT (NCT02405832) and a corroborating 2024 RCT on preoperative gargles for post-operative sore throat. However, a **blocking data gap** exists — local product safety labelling (warnings/contraindications) is entirely missing, which prevents this candidate from formally entering the S1 safety review stage.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Local safety labelling data (warnings, contraindications, drug interactions) — currently a blocking gap
- Detailed mechanism of action (MOA) documentation from DrugBank
- Confirmation of ARTG registration pathway, given zinc sulfate is not currently marketed in Australia
- A dose-ranging or confirmatory RCT specifically targeting acute pharyngitis (rather than post-operative sore throat or COVID-19 populations) to strengthen indication-specific evidence
