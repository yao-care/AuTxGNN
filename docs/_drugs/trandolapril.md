## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data for Trandolapril is not available in this evidence pack. Based on the information that is available (drawn from the model's own rationale text), Trandolapril is an ACE (angiotensin-converting enzyme) inhibitor. Its efficacy in blood pressure control has been well established as a class effect, and mechanistically ACE inhibitors are known to lower systemic blood pressure and provide renoprotective effects — which is the basis for the predicted link to malignant hypertensive renal disease.

Malignant hypertensive renal disease is a severe form of hypertension-related kidney injury. Since ACE inhibitors are a cornerstone therapy for blood pressure control and are widely used for renal protection in chronic kidney disease, there is a plausible theoretical rationale connecting Trandolapril's known pharmacology to this predicted indication. However, the evidence pack explicitly notes that this connection is a "pure prediction score" with no supporting clinical trials or literature — the mechanistic plausibility has not been tested for this specific, severe disease presentation.

It is worth noting that malignant hypertension with renal involvement is typically an acute, life-threatening presentation requiring rapid-acting IV antihypertensives and specialist inpatient management, which may differ substantially from Trandolapril's typical chronic oral dosing profile. This distinction has not been evaluated in the available data and represents an important caveat to the prediction.
