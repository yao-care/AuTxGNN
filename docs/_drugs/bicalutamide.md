## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on its known drug class, bicalutamide is a nonsteroidal androgen receptor (AR) antagonist.

Androgen signalling drives hair growth in androgen-sensitive follicles, so blocking the AR could plausibly reduce androgen-dependent hair growth. This is the mechanistic basis for the model's prediction.

The only supporting literature is a 2022 comment on a retrospective review of 35 patients, in which bicalutamide improved minoxidil-induced hypertrichosis in female pattern hair loss. That review itself is not among the records compiled for this report, so the evidence is indirect and secondary. It has not been confirmed in a controlled trial.

**Rationale:**

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
