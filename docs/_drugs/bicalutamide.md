## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Bicalutamide is, however, known as a nonsteroidal androgen receptor (AR) antagonist.

Androgen signalling drives hair growth in androgen-sensitive follicles, so blocking the AR could plausibly reduce androgen-dependent hair growth. This is a mechanistic hypothesis only. The one publication is a 2022 comment on a retrospective review of bicalutamide for minoxidil-induced hypertrichosis in female pattern hair loss. The source study is not among the records provided, and the comment gives no data of its own. It is indirect evidence and does not establish an effect of bicalutamide on hypertrichosis.
