## Sirolimus: Liposarcoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Sirolimus is an mTOR inhibitor marketed in Australia as an oral tablet. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**, with a score of **0.9989**. Support is limited: **1 completed single-arm Phase 2 trial** (in combination with cyclophosphamide) and **7 publications**, mostly reviews with indirect relevance, which places the evidence at **L3**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Sirolimus is known to inhibit mTORC1, and activation of the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway has been reported in soft tissue sarcomas, including liposarcoma. This gives a plausible biological reason to test an mTOR inhibitor in this tumour type.

The link is still indirect. The only trial is a single-arm study of sirolimus combined with cyclophosphamide, so the contribution of sirolimus alone cannot be separated from its partner drug. The supporting literature is mostly reviews and transplant cancer-risk studies.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only direct study is a single-arm Phase 2 trial in which sirolimus was combined with cyclophosphamide, with no results supplied and no randomised comparison. The remaining literature is indirect. This is a research question at evidence level L3, not yet a basis for clinical adoption.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Published efficacy results from NCT02821507, and ideally a randomised or controlled study of sirolimus in liposarcoma
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Safety information from the TGA-approved Product Information, including warnings and contraindications
- A review of the interactions listed above for the intended oncology setting
