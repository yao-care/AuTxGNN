# Ticagrelor: From Acute Coronary Syndrome to Intracranial Arteriosclerosis

## One-Sentence Summary

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action (MOA) data for ticagrelor is not available in this evidence pack. Based on the trial and literature context that is available, ticagrelor is an oral, reversible P2Y12 receptor antagonist used as a core antiplatelet agent in acute coronary syndrome and post-PCI settings. Its efficacy in preventing atherothrombotic events in these settings is well established (e.g. PLATO, THEMIS, TWILIGHT-type trials referenced elsewhere in this evidence pack), and mechanistically it may extend to intracranial arteriosclerosis, since this condition also involves platelet-mediated thrombotic narrowing of arteries.

Ticagrelor inhibits ADP-induced platelet activation and aggregation via reversible P2Y12 blockade. This mechanism has biological plausibility for the thrombotic stenosis/occlusion seen in intracranial atherosclerotic disease, consistent with its established mechanism in coronary and extracranial atherosclerosis. However, intracranial circulation has different haemodynamics from the coronary circulation, and bleeding risk — particularly intracranial haemorrhage — needs to be evaluated independently rather than assumed from coronary/peripheral data.

The strongest supporting trials (CAPTIVA, DREAM-PRIDE) are purpose-designed for intracranial atherosclerotic disease and directly compare antiplatelet/anticoagulant strategies including ticagrelor, which is why this indication scores well despite still being at an early evidence stage.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
While two purpose-built trials (CAPTIVA, DREAM-PRIDE) directly test antithrombotic strategies — including ticagrelor — in intracranial arteriosclerosis, neither has reported completed results, and evidence level is only L2. Ticagrelor is also not currently registered in Australia, and core safety documentation is missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- TGA-approved Product Information (key warnings, contraindications) — currently a blocking data gap
- Detailed mechanism of action documentation from DrugBank or the sponsor
- Completed results from CAPTIVA (NCT05047172) and DREAM-PRIDE (NCT04948749)
- Confirmation of any ARTG registration pathway if repurposing is pursued
- Intracranial-haemorrhage-specific safety and drug interaction data, given the differing bleeding risk profile of cerebral vs coronary circulation
