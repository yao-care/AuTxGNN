## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for selenium sulfide is not available in this evidence pack. Based on the information that is available (drawn from the rationale notes attached to other candidates in this pack), selenium sulfide is a topical antifungal and mild keratolytic agent, used mainly against Malassezia (Pityrosporum)-related skin conditions.

Vulvar inverted follicular keratosis is a rare, benign follicular tumour — a structural/proliferative lesion rather than a fungal or hyperkeratotic disorder of the type selenium sulfide is used to treat. The evidence pack's own rationale for this candidate is explicit: there is **no clear mechanistic link** between selenium sulfide's antifungal/antiproliferative action and this condition, and the TxGNN score reflects graph-similarity inference only, with no supporting clinical or preclinical data.

For context, a lower-ranked candidate in the same evidence pack — **cutaneous candidiasis** (rank 2, TxGNN score 83.86%) — has a more coherent mechanistic rationale (possible cross-reactivity between antifungal activity directed at Malassezia and Candida) and is backed by 2 review-level publications, though still without direct empirical (trial) data. This alternative may warrant closer attention than the top-ranked candidate discussed here.
