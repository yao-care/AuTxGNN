## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, ezetimibe inhibits NPC1L1-mediated intestinal cholesterol absorption and lowers LDL cholesterol.

CETP deficiency is characterised by raised HDL cholesterol and reduced LDL cholesterol. On this basis there is no clear therapeutic reason to add an LDL-lowering agent. The high score (0.9909) comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
