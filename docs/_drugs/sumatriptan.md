## Safety Considerations

**Contraindications suggested by the knowledge graph** (these are graph-derived associations, not label text):
- Cardiovascular and cerebrovascular: coronary artery disease, myocardial infarction, congestive heart failure, heart disease, cardiovascular disease, peripheral vascular disease, stroke, cerebral infarction, cerebrovascular disorder, hypertension
- Hepatic and renal: liver disease, liver failure, kidney disease
- Other: epilepsy, diabetes mellitus, diabetic ketoacidosis, substance abuse/dependence, blood coagulation disease, obesity, gallbladder disease, haemoglobinopathy and anaemia

**Drug interactions** (100 recorded; main ones listed):
- **Major:** dexfenfluramine, fenfluramine, lorcaserin, sibutramine (serotonergic agents), and 5-HT3 antagonists dolasetron, granisetron, ondansetron, palonosetron
- **Moderate:** morphine (including liposomal)

Other recorded interactions have unknown severity.

Other points from the literature and trial data: medication-overuse headache risk, and the theoretical vasoconstriction concern in brainstem aura. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for full warnings and contraindications.
