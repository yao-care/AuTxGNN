## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 326 interactions were identified in the interaction database, and all of those listed are rated Moderate. Examples include:
  - Sympathomimetics: epinephrine (including topical and ophthalmic forms), ephedrine, isometheptene, phentermine, diethylpropion, fenfluramine
  - QT-relevant agents: clarithromycin, cisapride, dolasetron, granisetron, palonosetron
  - Other agents: famotidine, loperamide, canagliflozin, ertugliflozin, semaglutide, albiglutide

A full medication review is advisable before use. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for warnings and contraindications.
