## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Bendamustine is an alkylating agent with a purine-analogue-like structure. It produces DNA cross-links and strand breaks. Detailed mechanism of action data are not otherwise available in the data compiled for this report.

Mantle cell lymphoma is a mature B-cell neoplasm, closely related to other B-cell lymphomas in which bendamustine is used. Bendamustine plus rituximab (BR) is an established backbone in mantle cell lymphoma, and Bruton tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitors are now being added to it in large randomised trials. This supports the plausibility of the TxGNN prediction.

- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found. Major-level interactions include deferiprone and samarium (153Sm) lexidronam (both add to myelosuppression risk). Moderate-level interactions include omeprazole, esomeprazole, cimetidine, caffeine, mercaptopurine, ticlopidine, chloramphenicol, deferasirox, roflumilast and osilodrostat, among others.
