## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Benzylpenicillin belongs to the penicillin class of antibiotics, which are generally active against streptococci and many oral anaerobic bacteria.

Pericoronitis is a polymicrobial infection (streptococci and anaerobes) of the tissue around a partially erupted tooth, usually a lower wisdom tooth. Benzylpenicillin's activity against these organisms makes the prediction biologically plausible. However, the link currently rests only on the model score. Oral penicillins such as penicillin V and amoxicillin are the agents typically used in dental infections, and parenteral benzylpenicillin is generally reserved for severe infection.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions** (21 recorded interactions): the most important is **methotrexate (Major)**, where penicillins can reduce methotrexate clearance and raise its toxicity. Moderate interactions include warfarin, dicoumarol, tetracycline-class antibiotics (doxycycline, minocycline, tetracycline, oxytetracycline, demeclocycline), chloramphenicol, ethinylestradiol, mycophenolic acid, picosulfuric acid, and radioactive iodide (I-123, I-131). Minor interactions include clarithromycin and erythromycin. Interactions of unknown severity are listed for salbutamol, acyclovir, gentamicin and levothyroxine.
