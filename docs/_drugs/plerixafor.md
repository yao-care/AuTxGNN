## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Based on known pharmacology, plerixafor is a CXCR4 antagonist (it also interacts with ACKR3). It blocks the CXCL12/CXCR4 axis, which anchors haematopoietic and malignant cells in the protective bone marrow niche, and so mobilises them into the circulation.

Plerixafor is already used to mobilise stem cells in multiple myeloma and non-Hodgkin lymphoma before autologous transplantation. The prediction for the indolent subtype extends this link to the disease itself. However, no trials or literature were found that support an anti-myeloma or disease-modifying role in indolent disease, so the prediction currently rests on the model score alone.

The strongest mechanistic case in the data is for **myeloid leukaemia**. Blocking CXCR4 may release leukaemic cells from the marrow niche and sensitise them to chemotherapy. This is supported by reviews (for example PMID 39261603 and 32877869) and by multiple early-phase combination trials.

## Cytotoxicity

Plerixafor is a haematopoietic stem cell mobiliser, not a cytotoxic anticancer drug, so a cytotoxicity assessment does not apply. When it is used alongside cytotoxic chemotherapy in leukaemia regimens, the handling and monitoring requirements of the partner drugs apply.

---

## Safety Considerations

Warnings, contraindications and label-based interactions are not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for safety information.

Additional points from the data:
- **Knowledge-graph contraindication signals** (not label text): thrombocytopenia, impaired renal function and leukaemia. These should be verified against the PI.
- **Pharmacological targets:** CXCR4 and ACKR3. No drug-drug interactions with other medicines were recorded.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Myeloid leukaemia has an L2 evidence level from Phase 1/2 trials only. It is better framed as a research question than a ready-to-adopt indication.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clinical or preclinical data on plerixafor in indolent myeloma or smouldering myeloma, or a decision to redirect the evaluation to myeloid leukaemia
- For myeloid leukaemia: randomised Phase 2/3 data, as current evidence is limited to small, early-phase combination studies with several terminated trials
- The approved indication text and safety information from the TGA PI for ARTG 10083Q
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Review of the renal impairment and thrombocytopenia signals before any use in a new population
