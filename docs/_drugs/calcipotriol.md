# Calcipotriol: From Plaque Psoriasis (Original Indication Not Recorded in ARTG Data) to Seborrheic Keratosis

## One-Sentence Summary

Calcipotriol is a topical vitamin D3 analogue, marketed in Australia in a combination foam with betamethasone (Enstilar). The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **seborrheic keratosis**, supported by **no registered clinical trials** and **4 publications**, which are mostly small case series and case reports.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, calcipotriol is a vitamin D3 analogue that binds the vitamin D receptor in keratinocytes. It inhibits proliferation, promotes differentiation and can induce apoptosis.

Seborrheic keratosis is a common benign, hyperproliferative epidermal lesion. Calcipotriol's established use in hyperproliferative keratotic skin disease makes an effect on these lesions mechanistically plausible. The original indication is not recorded in the ARTG data, so a direct comparison with the original indication cannot be made here.

Other predictions in the list, such as vulvar inverted follicular keratosis, probably follow from similarity to seborrheic keratosis. Predictions for conditions such as vulvovaginitis, bone Paget disease and breast fibrocystic disease have no clear mechanistic support and no evidence.

## Safety Considerations

No product-label warnings or contraindications are available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for safety information.

**Contraindication signals from the knowledge graph (not label text):** these relate to calcium and vitamin D handling and skin or vascular fragility. They include:
- Hypercalcaemia and other calcium metabolism disorders, nephrocalcinosis, calciphylaxis, hypervitaminosis
- Adrenocortical insufficiency and Addison disease
- Skin atrophy, telangiectasia and peripheral vascular disease
- Several ichthyoses, Darier disease and guttate psoriasis

**Drug interactions:** 84 potential interactions were identified, but severity levels are unclassified. Examples include corticosteroids (prednisone, prednisolone, hydrocortisone, triamcinolone), acetylsalicylic acid, metformin, simvastatin and clarithromycin. Clinical significance should be verified against the PI.

**Site-specific caution:** for genital or mucosal predictions (vulvitis, vulvovaginitis, atrophic vaginitis), local irritation and increased absorption are concerns.
