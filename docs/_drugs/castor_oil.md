## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Castor oil (rich in ricinoleic acid) is traditionally used topically as a hair emollient and in cosmetics. However, no evidence links it to hypotrichosis simplex, a rare hereditary hair-loss condition.

The high TxGNN score (0.981) is a graph-based prediction. It likely reflects shared hair-related annotations in the knowledge graph rather than a validated mechanism. Other top-ranked predictions are similar in nature: congenital hypotrichosis milia (98.0%) and diffuse alopecia areata (97.8%) also lack clinical support. Alopecia areata is autoimmune, and castor oil has no established immunomodulatory mechanism.

The only hair-related literature signal is for the broader indication **alopecia** (97.7%), described below. It is indirect, and no controlled clinical data exist for castor oil itself.
