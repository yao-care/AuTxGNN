## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed formal mechanism-of-action documentation (e.g. a DrugBank MOA record) was not retrievable for this candidate. However, the evidence pack's own repurposing analysis is explicit: Terbutaline is a selective β2-adrenergic agonist that acts directly on β2 receptors in airway smooth muscle to produce bronchodilation — this is a well-characterised, textbook pharmacological action, not an inferred or speculative one.

Because of this mechanism, the "predicted new indication" of Obstructive Lung Disease is not really novel — it corresponds to terbutaline's long-established clinical role as a rescue/relief bronchodilator in asthma and COPD (marketed internationally as Bricanyl®/Turbuhaler®). The TxGNN knowledge graph has essentially rediscovered an indication that is already standard practice overseas, which explains why the evidence base is unusually deep (L1, multiple completed Phase 3 RCTs) compared with the model's other, more speculative predictions for this drug (ranks 2–5 and 7–10, all L4–L5 with sparse or absent trial/literature support).

This has a practical implication for evaluators: the strength of evidence here reflects confirmation of known efficacy rather than discovery of a new therapeutic use, and should be weighted accordingly when comparing this candidate against genuinely novel repurposing signals elsewhere in the portfolio.
