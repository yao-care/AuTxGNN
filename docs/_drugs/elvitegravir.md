# Elvitegravir: From HIV Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Elvitegravir is an HIV-1 integrase inhibitor, marketed in Australia as part of the Genvoya combination tablet and used to treat HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, but there are currently **no registered clinical trials** and only **8 publications**, all preclinical (animal or laboratory) studies.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, elvitegravir belongs to the integrase strand transfer inhibitor (INSTI) class and is used as part of a fixed-dose combination with cobicistat, emtricitabine and tenofovir alafenamide. Its efficacy in HIV-1 infection is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to SIV.

HIV-1 and SIV are closely related lentiviruses with a conserved integrase active site. Laboratory studies show that integrase inhibitors are active against SIVmac239 and simian-tropic HIV, and that resistance mutations behave similarly in both viruses.

An important caveat: SIV infects non-human primates, so this "new indication" is relevant mainly as a translational research model for HIV, which is already the approved use. It does not represent a new human therapeutic indication. The other two TxGNN predictions (feline AIDS and a rare neurodevelopmental disorder) have no supporting studies and appear to be knowledge-graph artefacts; the feline indication is also veterinary and outside the scope of human drug repurposing.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The evidence is limited to in vitro and animal studies (L4), with no clinical trials. SIV infection occurs in non-human primates, so the prediction does not translate into a new human indication. It is useful mainly as a research model for HIV, where elvitegravir is already approved.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation that a human-relevant indication exists (SIV is not a human disease)
- Mechanism of action data for elvitegravir
- Warnings and contraindications from the TGA Product Information
- Approved indication text for the ARTG entry
- Clinical data, if any human use (for example, topical HIV prevention inserts) is to be pursued
