## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Docetaxel is a taxane. Taxanes stabilise microtubules and block cells from completing mitosis, so rapidly dividing tumour cells stop growing or die. Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank is not available, but this class effect is well established.

Breast carcinoma already appears among docetaxel's original indications in the knowledge graph. The prediction therefore reflects a known use, not a new one. The mechanism is the same cytotoxic action that underlies its use in other solid tumours. The model's high score is consistent with the large body of breast cancer trials and publications.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications (knowledge-graph associations, not label text):** liver disease, gallbladder disease, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, anaemia, hyperbilirubinaemia (including Gilbert syndrome), peripheral neuropathy and other neuritis, diarrhoeal disease, stomatitis, pulmonary oedema and cardiac tamponade.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified; the main ones are listed below.
  - **Major:** clarithromycin.
  - **Moderate:** aprepitant, rolapitant, cimetidine, metronidazole, tinidazole, miconazole, eliglustat, rosuvastatin, simvastatin, troglitazone.
  - **Minor:** dexamethasone, prednisolone, prednisone, levofloxacin.
- **Literature signals:** fluid retention and peripheral oedema, a possible association with breast cancer-related lymphedema, and rare interstitial pneumonitis.

TGA label warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for authoritative safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
- Docetaxel has extensive Phase 3 and Phase 2 evidence in breast cancer, including 10 completed Phase 3 trials of docetaxel-containing regimens, so the evidence level is L1.
- This is an existing use rather than true repurposing. The "Guardrails" apply because most trials test combinations and docetaxel carries significant myelosuppression and hepatic risks.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirm the TGA-approved indication wording for the ARTG entry against the Product Information.
- Obtain the label warnings and contraindications from the PI.
- Define the regimen and setting (adjuvant, neoadjuvant or metastatic; HER2 status) for any local use.
- Put in place a monitoring plan covering FBC, liver function, neuropathy and fluid retention, plus review of interacting medicines (especially strong CYP3A4 inhibitors such as clarithromycin).
- Other model predictions were weaker. Ewing sarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma are research questions only, and the remaining predictions are on hold for lack of evidence.
