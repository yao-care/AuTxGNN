## One-Sentence Summary

Dorzolamide is a topical carbonic anhydrase inhibitor, originally used to lower eye pressure in open-angle glaucoma and ocular hypertension.
The TxGNN model's top-ranked prediction is **hypotrichosis simplex of the scalp**, but **no clinical trials and no publications** specifically support it.
Of the ten predictions reviewed, only open-angle glaucoma has strong evidence (34 registered trials), and that is the drug's existing approved use rather than a new one.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for dorzolamide: "Carbonic anhydrase (CA) is an enzyme found in many tissues of the body including the eye. It catalyzes the reversible reaction involving the hydration of carbon dioxide and the dehydration of carbonic acid. […] Dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution contains dorzolamide hydrochloride, an inhibitor of human carbonic anhydrase II." ([DailyMed: Dorzolamide Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution (dorzolamide hydrochloride) prescribing information, Micro Labs Limited](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=4698e4e1-c5f6-4db1-9328-74464a85afea), label version 2026-11-14; accessed 2026-11-19.) Based on known pharmacology, dorzolamide inhibits carbonic anhydrase II in the ciliary processes. This reduces aqueous humour formation and lowers intraocular pressure. Pharmacology data also lists activity at carbonic anhydrase 1, 7, 12 and 14.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications flagged in the knowledge graph** (these are graph associations, not label text): diabetes mellitus, diabetic ketoacidosis, myasthenia gravis, neuromuscular junction disease, hyperthyroidism, Graves disease, thyrotoxicosis, hypoglycaemia, atrioventricular block, pulmonary emphysema, bronchial disease and anaphylaxis. Several of these resemble beta-blocker cautions, so they may partly reflect the timolol combination product. They should be checked against the Product Information.
- **Drug interactions**: the interaction query returned four pharmacological target interactions, all with carbonic anhydrase isoforms (CA1, CA7, CA12, CA14). These describe drug targets, not drug-drug interactions with severity ratings.

Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for full warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top-ranked prediction, hypotrichosis simplex of the scalp, rests on a model score alone, with no trials or publications. The hair and skin predictions have no plausible mechanism, and the cardiac and respiratory predictions have no efficacy evidence and little systemic exposure from eye drops.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Product Information warnings and contraindications from the TGA, plus the approved indication text for the Australian entry
- Published or registered studies of dorzolamide in primary hereditary (including paediatric and congenital) glaucoma
- Review of how much the knowledge-graph contraindications reflect the timolol combination rather than dorzolamide alone
