## Safety Considerations

Key warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for these.

**Drug Interactions** (100 interactions were identified; examples from the DDInter listing):
- **Major:** cisapride, dolasetron, eliglustat, loperamide
- **Moderate:** clarithromycin, levofloxacin, granisetron, metoclopramide, famotidine, fenfluramine, dexfenfluramine, eluxadoline, and several laxatives (bisacodyl, castor oil, lactulose, lactitol, glycerin, magnesium citrate, magnesium hydroxide)
- **Minor:** metronidazole

Mineralocorticoid excess from CYP17A1 inhibition can cause hypertension, fluid retention and hypokalaemia. This is a safety concern for any new use.
