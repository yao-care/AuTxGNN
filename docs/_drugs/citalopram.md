## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on its drug class, citalopram is a highly selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor, and its mechanism is inferred from that class rather than from a recorded mechanism entry.

Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) is known to respond to serotonergic medication. Reviews in the compiled literature describe serotonin reuptake inhibitors as a mainstay of pharmacological treatment. Citalopram-specific studies include a randomised open-label trial in treatment-resistant adult OCD, a single-blind comparison with fluvoxamine and paroxetine, a citalopram concentration–response study, and open-label trials in children and adolescents. Together these support a mechanistic and clinical link, although none is a placebo-controlled phase 3 trial.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Citalopram has direct, though limited, clinical evidence in OCD: one small single-blind comparison and several open-label studies, in adults and in children. There is no placebo-controlled phase 3 trial. The evidence level is L2, and the high TxGNN score (0.9974) is consistent with the clinical data.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Controlled trial data (ideally placebo-controlled) specific to citalopram in OCD
- Detailed mechanism of action data

- A monitoring plan for children, adolescents and patients taking interacting medicines
