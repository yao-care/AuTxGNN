## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Citalopram belongs to the SSRI class, which acts by blocking serotonin reuptake.

OCD is known to respond to serotonergic drugs, and SSRIs are widely used for it. The literature includes citalopram-specific work: an open-label randomised trial in treatment-resistant adult OCD, citalopram concentration–response data, open-label paediatric studies, and a single-blind comparison with fluvoxamine and paroxetine. Together these support a plausible mechanistic and clinical link, although the mechanism here is inferred from drug class.
