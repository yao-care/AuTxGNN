## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data was not returned by this Evidence Pack (flagged as a High-severity data gap). Based on established pharmacological knowledge, rasagiline is a selective, irreversible MAO-B inhibitor of the propargylamine class, approved for use in Parkinson's disease, where it reduces dopamine breakdown and has demonstrated neuroprotective/anti-apoptotic activity in preclinical models.

Lewy Body Dementia (LBD) and Parkinson's disease sit on the same neurodegenerative spectrum — both are **synucleinopathies** driven by pathological accumulation of α-synuclein. Literature in this pack (PMID 29769405) shows that α-synuclein directly binds and stimulates MAO-B activity, triggering downstream toxic cascades — a mechanistic bridge that makes MAO-B inhibition biologically plausible in LBD, not just PD.

However, the only trial identified for this indication (NCT05611372) targeted *prodromal* PD rather than LBD itself, was withdrawn, and enrolled zero participants. Supporting literature is largely mechanistic, imaging-based, or drawn from a related Phase II trial in Alzheimer's disease rather than LBD directly. The prediction is therefore best characterised as a well-reasoned research hypothesis rather than a clinically validated repurposing opportunity.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
While the mechanistic rationale linking MAO-B inhibition to α-synuclein pathology in Lewy Body Dementia is credible (Evidence Level L3), the only directly relevant clinical trial was withdrawn with zero enrolment, and the strongest clinical evidence (the Phase II RCT) was conducted in Alzheimer's disease, not LBD. This falls short of the threshold to proceed even with guardrails.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- TGA-approved Product Information (warnings, contraindications, drug interactions) — currently a Blocking data gap
- Confirmed mechanism-of-action documentation from DrugBank or equivalent source
- A completed or actively recruiting trial evaluating rasagiline specifically in a Lewy Body Dementia population
- Clarification of rasagiline's original approved indication and Australian regulatory status, given no ARTG entries were found in this pack
