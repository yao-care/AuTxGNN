## Cytotoxicity

Ublituximab is a targeted B-cell-depleting antibody, not a conventional cytotoxic chemotherapy, so a full cytotoxicity profile is not applicable. Please refer to the Product Information (PI) warnings and precautions for haematological monitoring and handling requirements.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The only interaction record is a pharmacology entry describing its target, CD20 (MS4A1). No drug-drug interactions with a severity level were identified.
- **Other considerations**: Anti-CD20 immunosuppression would carry infection risk. For a non-immune condition such as age-related cataract, this offers no supporting benefit.

Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for warnings and contraindications.
