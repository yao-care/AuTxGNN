## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions** (5 interactions identified in the DDInter database):
  - **Major:** Dimercaprol
  - **Moderate:** Iron, ferrous fumarate, ferrous gluconate, chloramphenicol

Warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for these.

Concerns noted for this prediction include possible iron-related oxidative stress in the retina. Infusion-related hypersensitivity and skin reactions are also a recognised consideration for intravenous iron.
