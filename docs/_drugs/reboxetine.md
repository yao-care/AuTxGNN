## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Structured mechanism-of-action data is not available for this candidate (DrugBank MOA field flagged as a data gap). Based on the literature within this evidence pack, reboxetine is a selective noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor (NRI) that blocks reuptake of extracellular norepinephrine at the norepinephrine transporter, and was developed as an antidepressant with demonstrated efficacy in major depressive disorder.

The noradrenergic system plays a recognised role in the autonomic arousal response underlying panic attacks, and agoraphobia commonly co-occurs with, or develops secondary to, panic disorder. Two open-label studies included in this evidence pack (Versiani 2002, PMID 11838623; Dannon 2002, PMID 12415550) tested reboxetine directly in panic disorder patients — including SSRI treatment-refractory cases — and reported it to be effective and well tolerated, giving a plausible mechanistic and clinical bridge to agoraphobia.

However, no study in this evidence pack tests reboxetine specifically in a formally diagnosed agoraphobia population; the supporting literature is indirect (derived from panic disorder trials and broader anxiety-disorder reviews), and no clinical trials or ANZCTR/ICTRP registrations exist for this drug-disease pairing. This gap between mechanistic plausibility and disease-specific validation is the basis for the L3 evidence rating.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Evidence for agoraphobia is indirect — derived from panic disorder studies rather than agoraphobia-specific trials — and limited to open-label studies and reviews (L3), with no registered clinical trials. Reboxetine is also not currently registered in Australia (0 ARTG entries), and safety/contraindication data are a documented **Blocking** data gap, precluding entry into initial safety assessment.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Direct clinical evidence (ideally RCT) in a formally diagnosed agoraphobia population, not just panic disorder
- TGA Product Information / safety, warnings and contraindication data (currently a Blocking data gap)
- Confirmed mechanism of action documentation (currently a data gap)
- Assessment of regulatory pathway for a non-ARTG-registered drug in Australia
