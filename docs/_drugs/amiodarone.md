## Amiodarone: Catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Amiodarone is an oral antiarrhythmic that blocks several cardiac ion channels and has anti-adrenergic effects. The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia (CPVT)**. No clinical trials are registered, and the 3 CPVT cohort or review papers retrieved do not show that amiodarone works for this condition, so the evidence is weak.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Amiodarone is generally described as a multi-channel blocker: it acts on potassium, sodium and calcium channels and has beta-adrenergic antagonist properties. These actions suppress ventricular arrhythmias.

CPVT is a rare inherited arrhythmia caused mainly by RYR2 or CASQ2 mutations. These mutations cause calcium leak from the sarcoplasmic reticulum, and adrenergic stress (exercise or emotion) then triggers arrhythmia. Amiodarone's anti-adrenergic and calcium-related effects are therefore plausibly relevant.

There are important caveats:
- Amiodarone is not a first-line option for CPVT. Beta-blockers and flecainide are the standard drug therapies.
- The retrieved literature consists of cohort studies, a review and case reports. None shows amiodarone efficacy in CPVT.
- The high model score is a computational signal, not clinical proof.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high (0.9978), but no trials are registered and no retrieved paper shows amiodarone benefit in CPVT. Established first-line options (beta-blockers, flecainide) already exist, and amiodarone carries significant long-term toxicity.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clinical data on amiodarone specifically in CPVT, such as case series or comparative studies against beta-blockers or flecainide
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Review of TGA Product Information warnings and contraindications
- A defined role for amiodarone in CPVT, for example refractory cases after first-line therapy, with a toxicity monitoring plan
