## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Cetirizine belongs to the H1-receptor antagonist (antihistamine) class. The general literature describes competitive blockade of histamine H1-receptors on nerve endings, smooth muscle and glandular cells as the main way these drugs act in allergic disease.

"Nasal cavity disease" is a broad term. Much of it overlaps with allergic and vasomotor nasal conditions where H1 antagonism is already relevant. This is why the model score is high. However, the retrieved literature is general or indirect. It does not offer distinct evidence that cetirizine benefits a new nasal condition beyond those already covered.
