Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, choriogonadotropin alfa acts on LH/hCG receptors in gonadal tissue and is used for ovulation induction and support of spermatogenesis.

No established biological pathway links LH-receptor agonism to oesophageal mucosal injury or reflux. The high score (0.984) reflects proximity in a knowledge graph, not biological or clinical evidence. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis-generating signal rather than a reasoned repurposing case.

All are Level L5, with no trials or literature and no plausible mechanistic rationale identified.
