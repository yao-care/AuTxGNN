## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available for this report. Based on known information, olanzapine is a second-generation antipsychotic whose efficacy in schizophrenia and bipolar disorder is established. Its receptor profile (5-HT2A/2C, D2 and H1 antagonism) may modulate mood and fear circuits.

**Rank 1 (benign paroxysmal torticollis of infancy):** Only the model score supports this. The condition is a paediatric, probably channelopathy-related migraine equivalent, and no plausible olanzapine mechanism is evident. Antipsychotic use in infants would also raise significant safety concerns.

**Agoraphobia (rank 2):** Agoraphobia is closely tied to panic disorder. A 12-week open-label trial of low-dose olanzapine (5 mg/day) added to an SSRI in 31 patients with SSRI-resistant panic disorder, with or without agoraphobia, supports a possible role, along with case reports. The evidence is indirect, uncontrolled and small.

**Neurotic depression and melancholia (ranks 6 and 7):** Both fall within the major depressive disorder spectrum, which is already an original indication for olanzapine. Several systematic reviews and network meta-analyses support antipsychotic augmentation in treatment-resistant depression, and the olanzapine-fluoxetine combination is established in this setting. No studies specific to either diagnosis were supplied.

**Dysthymic disorder (rank 3):** Evidence is limited to a general review of second-generation antipsychotics and an open-label study in borderline personality disorder with comorbid dysthymia. No dysthymia-specific RCT of olanzapine was identified.

**Ranks 4, 9 and 10:** These are prediction only, with no mechanistic rationale supported by the data.

## Safety Considerations

**Contraindications and cautions (from knowledge graph disease associations, not product label text):**
The knowledge graph links olanzapine to cautions or contraindications in several groups of conditions:
- **Metabolic:** diabetes mellitus, hyperglycaemia, diabetic ketoacidosis, hyperlipidaemia, hypertriglyceridaemia, obesity
- **Cardiovascular and cerebrovascular:** stroke, cerebrovascular disorder, cerebral infarction, myocardial infarction, orthostatic hypotension, hypotensive disorders
- **Hepatic and haematological:** liver disease, cirrhosis, liver failure, gallbladder disease, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, blood coagulation disease
- **Other:** epilepsy, benign prostatic hyperplasia, female breast carcinoma, multiple system atrophy, postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome

**Drug interactions:** 100 interactions were identified. The main ones include:
- **Major:** bupropion, morphine, potassium citrate
- **Moderate:** antidiabetic agents (acarbose, metformin, alogliptin, albiglutide, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, chlorpropamide), anticholinergics (atropine, hyoscyamine, glycopyrronium), clarithromycin, famotidine, epinephrine, loperamide, lorcaserin, and bowel preparations

Metabolic risk is also documented in the literature, with a meta-analysis showing high rates of metabolic syndrome in people with psychotic and mood disorders (PMID 26407790). Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for full warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold for the rank 1 prediction (benign paroxysmal torticollis of infancy). Proceed with Guardrails for neurotic depression and melancholia. Agoraphobia and dysthymic disorder remain research questions.**

**Rationale:**
- The rank 1 prediction rests only on a model score, with no trials, no literature and no plausible mechanism, and infant exposure to an antipsychotic raises safety concerns.
- Neurotic depression and melancholia fall within the depression spectrum, where antipsychotic augmentation is supported by multiple systematic reviews. However, these are indirect and not specific to olanzapine or to these diagnoses, and no registered trials were supplied. Because they overlap with an existing indication, they offer little genuinely new repurposing value.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data and the PI warnings and contraindications (not available in the data compiled for this report)
- Approved indication text for ARTG 3381Y
- Controlled trials of olanzapine in agoraphobia or panic disorder, and in dysthymia
- A metabolic, weight and sedation monitoring plan for depression and anxiety populations, limiting use to treatment-resistant cases
- Review of interaction risks, particularly with antidiabetic agents, bupropion and morphine
