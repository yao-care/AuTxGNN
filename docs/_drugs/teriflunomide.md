## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The data compiled for this report list 100 interactions, with 20 shown. Most flagged are Major, including:
  - Corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, triamcinolone)
  - Antidiabetic agents (acarbose, pioglitazone)
  - Bupropion, acetylsalicylic acid, clarithromycin, minocycline
  - Chenodeoxycholic acid, oxandrolone, oxymetholone
- Moderate interactions include alosetron, cimetidine, empagliflozin and sitagliptin. Minor interactions include zinc sulfate and zinc acetate.
- **Additional guardrails noted in the analysis**: monitor for hepatotoxicity, avoid use in pregnancy because of teratogenicity, and use the accelerated elimination procedure when required. Haematological adverse effects are a recognised concern.

Product-specific warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for full safety information.
