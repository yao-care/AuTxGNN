## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for brigatinib: "Brigatinib is a tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) with in vitro activity at clinically achievable concentrations against multiple kinases including ALK, ROS1, insulin-like growth factor-1 receptor (IGF-1R), and FLT-3 as well as EGFR deletion and point mutations. Brigatinib inhibited autophosphorylation of ALK and ALK-mediated phosphorylation of the downstream signaling proteins STAT3, AKT, ERK1/2, and S6 in in vitro and in vivo assays." ([DailyMed: Alunbrig (brigatinib) prescribing information, Takeda Pharmaceuticals America, Inc.](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=0fe9ff20-d402-41f3-bc1e-7002ea7007db), label version 2026-01-12; accessed 2026-11-19.) Brigatinib is a tyrosine kinase inhibitor, and published work shows it inhibits several kinases beyond ALK in other settings (for example, NF2-deficient tumour models).

The data compiled for this report identify no mechanistic link between brigatinib and gingival fibromatosis. The score of 0.9989 is a model-generated prediction only. Any rationale, such as kinase inhibition affecting fibroblast overgrowth, would be speculative and is not supported by the data. This prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
