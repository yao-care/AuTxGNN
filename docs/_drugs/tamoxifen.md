# Tamoxifen: From Breast Cancer to Mammary Paget Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Tamoxifen is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM) established for the treatment and prevention of hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. The TxGNN model predicts potential efficacy in **Mammary Paget Disease**, but this direction is currently supported by only **1 clinical trial** (indirectly relevant) and **13 publications**, the majority of which are case reports rather than direct interventional evidence.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for Tamoxifen is not available in this Evidence Pack (Data Gap DG002). Based on well-established pharmacological knowledge, Tamoxifen is a SERM that competitively antagonises the estrogen receptor in breast tissue, and its efficacy in ER-positive breast cancer is well proven.

Mammary Paget disease is a rare malignancy of the nipple-areola complex that, in the majority of cases, coexists with an underlying ER-positive ductal carcinoma. Clinical management typically follows the treatment paradigm for the associated breast cancer rather than targeting Paget cells specifically.

Mechanistically, the rationale for Tamoxifen in this setting is indirect: it is presumed to act on the underlying ER-positive carcinoma component rather than on Paget cells themselves. As the repurposing rationale for this candidate notes, there is no dedicated interventional trial testing Tamoxifen against mammary Paget disease directly — the single Phase 3 trial identified (NCT00002920) tested a different intervention (medroxyprogesterone acetate) in a population that happened to include Paget's disease patients on background Tamoxifen, and the supporting literature is dominated by case reports rather than controlled studies.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only clinical trial identified does not test Tamoxifen against mammary Paget disease directly (population overlap only, relevance grade C), and the literature base consists almost entirely of case reports and small case series rather than controlled studies. Combined with a Blocking data gap on TFDA/PI safety information and no confirmed Australian market presence, this candidate does not yet meet the threshold to proceed.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- TGA-approved Product Information (warnings, contraindications, DDI) to close the Blocking data gap (DG001)
- Confirmed mechanism-of-action data for Tamoxifen (DG002)
- A dedicated case series, registry study, or interventional trial evaluating Tamoxifen specifically in mammary (not extramammary) Paget disease
- Clarification of current ARTG/market status in Australia, given this Evidence Pack shows 0 active entries
