## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Vildagliptin is a DPP-4 inhibitor. The compiled pharmacology data lists DPP-4 as its main target, along with DPP-8, DPP-9 and TRPV4. Its efficacy in type 2 diabetes is established. It raises incretin levels (GLP-1 and GIP), which supports glucose-dependent insulin secretion and reduces glucagon.

The data compiled for this report does not support a mechanistic link between this drug and classic stiff person syndrome. Stiff person syndrome is a rare autoimmune neurological disorder, often associated with anti-GAD65 antibodies. No established connection to DPP-4 inhibition was identified. The very high TxGNN score (99.88%) reflects proximity in a knowledge graph only. It is not evidence of benefit. A closely related condition, focal stiff limb syndrome, received an identical score and also has no supporting evidence.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on a graph-based score alone (Evidence Level L5). There are no clinical trials or publications, and no plausible mechanism linking DPP-4 inhibition to stiff person syndrome. The original mechanism of action data and the product safety information are also missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A credible mechanistic hypothesis connecting DPP-4 inhibition to the pathophysiology of stiff person syndrome, such as GAD65 autoimmunity or GABAergic dysfunction
- Preclinical or case-level evidence in stiff person syndrome
- Detailed mechanism of action data (MOA)
- TGA Product Information warnings and contraindications for a safety review
- The approved indication text for the ARTG entry

**Note:** Among the other predictions in the data compiled, **type 1 diabetes mellitus** (score 99.37%) has substantially more support. It is rated L2, but the evidence is small and adjunctive, and vildagliptin is not approved for type 1 diabetes.
