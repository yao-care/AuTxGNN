## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data from the original drug record is not available. Based on the analysis compiled for this report, tioguanine is a thiopurine antimetabolite. It is incorporated into DNA and RNA as thioguanine nucleotides, which disrupts nucleic acid synthesis and triggers mismatch-repair-mediated cytotoxicity in rapidly dividing myeloid cells.

Its established use in AML provides a plausible biological link to other myeloid malignancies, including CML, particularly blast phase disease, which often behaves like acute leukaemia. However, modern CML care is dominated by BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase inhibitors. Any role for tioguanine would therefore be historical or limited to refractory or blast-phase settings. The very high TxGNN score is a model output and is not backed by clinical data here.
