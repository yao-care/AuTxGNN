# Bleomycin: From Lymphoma and Head and Neck Cancer to Reticulum Cell Sarcoma (Lymphoma)

## One-Sentence Summary

Bleomycin is a DNA-damaging cytotoxic antibiotic, originally used for Hodgkin and non-Hodgkin lymphomas and head and neck cancer.
The TxGNN model's top-ranked prediction is **cauda equina neoplasm**, but it has no clinical support. The best-evidenced prediction is **reticulum cell sarcoma** (an obsolete term for aggressive non-Hodgkin lymphoma), supported by **11 clinical trials** (4 Phase 3) and **20 publications**, and this is largely an existing indication rather than true repurposing.

> **Note on selection:** The top-ranked prediction (rank 1, cauda equina neoplasm, score 99.30%) has only one off-topic paper and no trials (evidence level L5, Hold). This report therefore centres on the rank 3 prediction, reticulum cell sarcoma, which has the strongest evidence. Other predictions are summarised in the table below.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Bleomycin is a cytotoxic agent that causes DNA strand breaks, and its efficacy in Hodgkin and non-Hodgkin lymphoma is well established. Mechanistically, it is applicable to other lymphoid malignancies.

"Reticulum cell sarcoma" is an obsolete name for diffuse large B-cell/non-Hodgkin lymphoma. Bleomycin already covers non-Hodgkin lymphoma and lymphoma among its original indications, so this prediction is largely on-label or label-adjacent. Bleomycin-containing regimens (ProMACE-CytaBOM, PMitCEBO, VACOP-B and others) have been tested in aggressive and HIV-related lymphoma. Early reports from 1971 and 1972 also describe single-agent bleomycin activity in reticulum cell sarcoma and mycosis fungoides.

A caveat is that the Phase 3 trials test combination regimens, so bleomycin's own contribution is not isolated.

## Safety Considerations

- **Pulmonary toxicity:** The linked literature repeatedly identifies bleomycin pulmonary toxicity as a major risk, with interstitial pneumonitis, increased risk with advancing age, and enhanced toxicity when combined with radiotherapy. This matters particularly for primary pulmonary lymphoma and lung tumours.
- **Hyperpyrexia:** An acute, occasionally fatal hyperpyrexic reaction has been reported (PMID 6187214), and test dosing is traditionally recommended.
- **Drug interactions:** An interaction search returned 100 records. Of the 20 shown, 1 was classed Minor (levofloxacin) and 19 had no severity grading (for example pantoprazole, omeprazole, prednisone, vancomycin, morphine, ondansetron and clarithromycin). The listing does not support specific clinical conclusions.

Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for full warnings and contraindications, as these were not available in the data compiled for this report.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails** (for reticulum cell sarcoma/aggressive lymphoma only)

**Rationale:**
- Several completed Phase 3 trials and multiple Phase 2 trials use bleomycin-containing regimens in aggressive lymphoma, and bleomycin is already used in lymphoma. This is largely a label-adjacent indication, and bleomycin's individual contribution cannot be separated from the combination regimens.
- The other predictions are weaker. The top-ranked cauda equina neoplasm has no supporting evidence and should be held. High-grade glioma (L3) and interdigitating dendritic cell sarcoma (L4, case reports of response to ABVD) are research questions only.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Product Information (PI) warnings and contraindications for the Australian product, and its approved indication text
- Mechanism of action data
- Confirmation of the modern histological classification of "reticulum cell sarcoma" (for example diffuse large B-cell lymphoma) and a check against current Australian treatment guidelines
- A pulmonary toxicity monitoring plan (age, cumulative dose, radiotherapy exposure, baseline lung function)
- For the Hold and Research Question predictions, new prospective data before any further consideration
