## Magnesium Sulfate: Thrombotic disease
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Magnesium sulfate is a magnesium salt that has long been marketed in Australia. The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **thrombotic disease**. Support is limited to **1 clinical trial** (a Phase 3 study in thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura, with status unknown and no results available) and a small body of mostly animal and early human literature.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available for this drug. The following reasoning is drawn from the published studies compiled for this report.

Animal studies from 1980 to 2001 suggest that magnesium sulfate reduces platelet deposition and thrombus formation after vascular injury. In a rat carotid thrombosis model, intravenous magnesium prevented or delayed clot formation, and the authors also noted impaired coagulation. Magnesium acts as a physiological calcium antagonist, so it may modulate calcium-dependent coagulation, platelet activation and vascular tone.

Human data are sparse. They consist of one small non-randomised pilot study in patients receiving coronary stents, and one Phase 3 randomised trial in thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP). No confirmatory human efficacy evidence is available, so the prediction remains hypothesis-generating.

- **Moderate:** doxycycline, tetracycline and ciprofloxacin, as well as bisphosphonates (alendronic acid, risedronic acid, ibandronate), nifedipine and amlodipine, cisatracurium, corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone), and vitamin D analogues (cholecalciferol, calcifediol, calcitriol)

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The model score is very high, but the only clinical trial targets a single rare condition (TTP), has unknown status and has no results. The remaining support is mostly animal work from the 1980s and 1990s plus one small pilot study. This is not enough to justify progressing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Results or status confirmation for NCT03237819, and a decision on which thrombotic condition (for example TTP, stent thrombosis or venous thromboembolism) to pursue
- Controlled human studies with thrombotic endpoints and defined dosing
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- A review of the TGA Product Information for safety and interaction guidance, particularly for the major interactions listed above
