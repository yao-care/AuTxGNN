## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Iron is required for erythropoiesis and DNA synthesis, which is the general rationale for using iron salts in anaemias caused by low iron.

The link to the predicted disease is weak. Megaloblastic anaemia that does not depend on vitamin B12 or folate is typically caused by a defect in nucleotide metabolism or another genetic cause, not by a lack of iron. A causal relationship with iron supplementation has therefore not been established. The high score should be treated as a model signal that needs expert review, not as evidence of benefit.
