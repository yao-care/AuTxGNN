## Safety Considerations

Warnings and contraindications from the Australian Product Information were not available in the compiled data. Please refer to the TGA-approved Product Information (PI) for these.

**Drug Interactions:** The interaction screen identified 75 potential interacting drugs. The 20 entries reviewed were all rated **Moderate**. The main groups were:
- **Proton pump inhibitors:** omeprazole, esomeprazole, lansoprazole, dexlansoprazole, pantoprazole, rabeprazole
- **Antacids and mineral products:** aluminium hydroxide, magaldrate, calcium carbonate, magnesium oxide, magnesium carbonate, magnesium hydroxide
- **SGLT2 inhibitors:** canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin
- **Others:** clarithromycin, dexamethasone, mannitol, papaverine
