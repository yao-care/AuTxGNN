## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Riociguat is known to be an sGC stimulator. It increases cyclic GMP (cGMP) signalling, which produces pulmonary vasodilation and antiproliferative effects. This mechanism underlies its use in PAH and CTEPH.

No mechanistic link to Ambras type hypertrichosis universalis congenita has been established. This condition is a genetic disorder of hair growth, and no known pathway connects sGC-cGMP signalling to it. The high score (0.949) most likely reflects similarity patterns in the underlying knowledge graph rather than biological rationale.

There is also a direction-of-effect concern. Excess hair growth is a recognised adverse effect of some other vasodilators, such as minoxidil. A predicted benefit in a hypertrichosis disorder is therefore questionable. The same pattern appears among the other top-ranked predictions, which are mostly developmental or hair-related conditions with no supporting data.
